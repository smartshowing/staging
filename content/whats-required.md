---
title: "What's Required"
---

To operate effectively, the Smart Showing Kit requires only a few basic conditions.  
No technical setup or ongoing involvement is required from the seller.

## Requirements

- **Active home internet (Wi-Fi)**  
  Used for system connectivity, alerts, and remote management.

- **Standard household power**  
  Select devices use existing outlets; no electrical work is required.

- **Temporary access to the property**  
  For professional installation, testing, and removal.

- **Owner consent during the listing period**  
  Devices are installed only for the agreed showing or vacancy window.

- **Defined listing or showing timeframe**  
  The system is active only while the property is listed or as requested.

## Not Required

- No permanent wiring  
- No property modifications  
- No seller-managed apps or dashboards  
- No technical knowledge or configuration  

All setup, monitoring, and removal are handled as part of the service.
