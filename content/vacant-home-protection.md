---
title: "Vacant Home Protection"
---

Vacant homes face a different set of risks than occupied properties. Without regular presence, issues can go unnoticed for extended periods of time.

The Smart Showing Kit is designed to provide continuous awareness during these periods, helping to identify unexpected activity early.

## Continuous Coverage

When the property is vacant, the system operates in a protective state focused on early detection. Awareness is maintained around entry points and selected interior zones to ensure the home remains accounted for between showings.

This coverage is active at all times when the property is unoccupied.

## Early Detection

Unexpected entry or interior activity during vacant periods is treated differently than movement during an active showing. The system is designed to recognize this distinction and respond according to its current mode.

Early awareness allows issues to be addressed before they escalate.

## Between Showings

Vacant protection is not limited to overnight hours. It applies continuously between scheduled showings, open houses, and visits.

This ensures the property remains protected even during extended gaps in activity.

## Peace of Mind for Sellers and Agents

By maintaining awareness during vacant periods, the system helps reduce uncertainty for both sellers and agents. It provides reassurance that the property is being observed and that unexpected situations will not go unnoticed.

The result is a calmer, more controlled listing process.
