---
title: "Security & Liability Considerations"
---

Real estate showings introduce risk—particularly when properties are vacant or access is granted without an agent present. The Smart Showing Kit is designed to reduce uncertainty and support responsible oversight throughout the listing period.

This section outlines how the system helps manage security expectations and liability considerations for both sellers and agents.

## Accountability Without Intrusion

By establishing clear entry and exit awareness, the system creates a record of when access occurs without requiring constant on-site supervision.

This helps:
- Confirm that showings occur as scheduled
- Reduce ambiguity around access
- Provide clarity if questions arise after a visit

The system supports accountability without interfering with the showing experience.

## Reduced Need for Physical Presence

Remote confirmation of access and departure reduces the need for agents to repeatedly travel to and from the property.

Fewer on-site visits can help:
- Lower exposure to risk
- Reduce time spent unlocking, waiting, or rechecking the property
- Improve efficiency across multiple listings

## Vacant Property Awareness

Vacant properties carry increased exposure to unauthorized entry, damage, or unnoticed issues.

By maintaining awareness during vacant periods, the system helps identify unexpected activity early, allowing concerns to be addressed before they escalate.

## Clear Scope and Disclosure

The Smart Showing Kit is a temporary, managed system with a clearly defined scope.

Key points include:
- Devices are disclosed as part of the service
- Monitoring is limited to non-private areas
- The system is active only during the agreed period

This clarity helps set appropriate expectations for all parties.

## Not a Substitute for Insurance or Legal Counsel

The system is designed to support the listing process, not replace insurance coverage, legal agreements, or required disclosures.

It provides operational awareness and support, but does not guarantee outcomes or eliminate all risk.

## Professional Management

All aspects of the system—including installation, configuration, monitoring, and removal—are managed as part of the service.

This reduces the likelihood of misconfiguration or misuse and ensures the system operates consistently with its intended purpose.
