title: "Notifications & Response"
---

For a remote showing system to save time, it must do more than detect activity—it must communicate clearly and only when necessary.

The Smart Showing Kit is designed to notify agents when it matters, without creating noise or requiring constant attention.

## Purpose

Notifications provide real-time awareness without requiring the agent to be on site. They confirm that a showing is proceeding as expected and highlight exceptions that may require action.

This allows agents to remain productive while maintaining oversight across multiple listings.

## What Triggers Notifications

Notifications are generated for key events, including:
- Entry into the property
- Exit from the property
- Activity occurring outside expected showing windows
- Unexpected access during vacant periods

Routine movement during an active showing does not generate unnecessary alerts.

## How Notifications Are Delivered

Notifications are delivered remotely through agreed communication channels.

They are:
- Brief and informational
- Event-based, not continuous
- Designed to be acknowledged quickly

No live monitoring or constant attention is required.

## Agent Response

In most cases, no response is needed. Notifications serve as confirmation that the system is operating as intended.

When action is required, the agent can:
- Verify entry or exit remotely
- Confirm that the property has been vacated
- Decide whether follow-up is necessary

This eliminates many return trips that would otherwise be required.

## Vacant Period Alerts

During vacant periods, notifications are more sensitive. Unexpected entry or interior activity generates immediate awareness so that issues can be addressed early.

This protects the property without requiring physical checks between showings.

## Managed as Part of the Service

Notification rules and responses are managed as part of the service. Agents do not need to configure alerts, adjust thresholds, or manage technical settings.

The system is designed to support the agent—not demand attention.
