---
title: "Interior Zones"
---

Interior zones extend system awareness beyond the entry point into key living areas of the home. These zones are selected to reflect how the property is actually used during a showing.

The goal is not blanket coverage, but focused awareness in primary spaces.

## Purpose

Interior zones provide context for activity within the home. By monitoring selected areas, the system can distinguish between normal showing movement and unexpected behavior.

Zones are chosen to balance visibility with discretion, supporting the showing experience rather than interrupting it.

## Zone Selection

Interior zones typically include:
- Living rooms or family rooms
- Main common areas
- Central walkways between rooms

Private spaces such as bedrooms and bathrooms are not used as interior zones.

## During a Showing

While a showing is in progress, interior zones support awareness without drawing attention. Movement through these areas is expected and interpreted within the context of an active visit.

The system remains passive, allowing agents and visitors to move naturally through the space.

## When the Property Is Vacant

When the home is unoccupied, interior zones play a critical role in early detection. Activity in these areas outside of expected windows is treated differently and handled according to the system's current mode.

This helps ensure that unexpected entry or movement is identified promptly.

## Coordinated Awareness

Interior zones work together with the entry / exit area to provide a complete picture of property use. Entry establishes intent, interior zones provide context, and exit confirms completion.

Together, they form a coherent framework for understanding activity during the listing period.
