---
title: "Who This Is For"
---

The Smart Showing Kit is designed for listings where time, distance, or limited on-site presence make traditional showing practices inefficient or impractical.

It is not intended for every property. It is most effective in situations where remote capability and continuous awareness provide clear value.

## Vacant Listings

Vacant properties benefit most from continuous awareness between showings.

The system helps:
- Reduce uncertainty during idle periods
- Identify unexpected access early
- Minimize unnecessary property checks

This is especially valuable when a property is unoccupied for extended periods.

## Remote Sellers

For sellers who are not local, visibility into property access provides reassurance without requiring frequent updates or physical presence.

The system allows:
- Showings to proceed without coordination delays
- Questions about access to be answered quickly
- The property to remain protected between visits

## Busy Agents Managing Multiple Listings

Agents handling multiple properties benefit from reduced travel and fewer routine site visits.

The system supports:
- Remote confirmation of showings
- Fewer unlock-and-wait scenarios
- More efficient use of time across listings

## Higher-Value or Sensitive Properties

Listings that require additional oversight—due to value, contents, or location—benefit from controlled access and awareness.

The system adds structure without introducing friction into the showing process.

## Situations Where It May Not Be Needed

The Smart Showing Kit may not be necessary for:
- Owner-occupied homes
- Listings with constant on-site supervision
- Short-term listings with minimal showing activity

The service is intended to be applied where it provides clear operational value.
