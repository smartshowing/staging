title: "Getting Started"
---

The Smart Showing Kit is designed to be simple to deploy and easy to use.  
Getting started requires coordination—not technical setup—from the agent or seller.

## Initial Consultation

The process begins with a brief consultation to determine whether the system is appropriate for the listing.

This includes:
- Understanding the property layout
- Reviewing the listing timeline
- Identifying showing patterns and needs
- Confirming eligibility and expectations

This ensures the system is applied only where it provides clear value.

## Scheduling Installation

Once approved, installation is scheduled at a convenient time prior to the listing or showing period.

Installation is handled professionally and includes:
- On-site setup
- System testing
- Confirmation that the system is operating as intended

No preparation is required from the seller.

## During the Listing

Throughout the active period:
- The system operates in the appropriate mode
- Notifications and responses are managed as part of the service
- Adjustments are made as conditions change

Agents and sellers are supported without needing to manage the system themselves.

## Ending the Service

When the listing concludes or the service window ends:
- The system is removed
- Devices are taken down
- Temporary operational data is cleared

The property is returned to its original condition.

## Next Steps

To determine whether the Smart Showing Kit is a fit for a specific listing, contact your service provider to schedule an initial discussion.

This conversation will confirm scope, timing, and expectations before moving forward.
