---
title: "Data Handling & Retention"
---

The Smart Showing Kit is designed to support showings and property protection without collecting or retaining unnecessary information.

Data handling is intentionally limited, purpose-driven, and aligned with the temporary nature of the service.

## Guiding Principles

The system follows three core principles:

- **Minimal collection** — only what is needed to operate the system
- **Event-based use** — information is tied to specific events, not continuous monitoring
- **Temporary retention** — data is not stored beyond its usefulness

## What Information Is Used

During operation, the system may generate short-lived information related to:
- Entry and exit events
- System state changes
- Notifications delivered to the agent

This information is used to confirm activity and support remote oversight.

## What Is Not Collected

The system is intentionally designed to avoid collecting:
- Audio recordings
- Continuous video recordings
- Personal data about visitors
- Activity in private areas

Bedrooms, bathrooms, and other private spaces are never monitored.

## Retention During the Listing Period

Information generated during the listing period is retained only as needed to:
- Support active showings
- Resolve recent questions or confirmations
- Maintain system reliability

There is no long-term accumulation of historical data.

## Removal at End of Service

When the system is removed:
- Devices are physically taken down
- Temporary operational data is cleared
- No ongoing monitoring remains

The property returns to its original state with no residual system presence.

## Transparency and Disclosure

The presence of devices and their general purpose are disclosed as part of the service. The system is designed to be transparent, predictable, and respectful of all parties.
