## Exterior vs Interior Zones

### Exterior Entry / Exit Zone

The **Exterior Entry / Exit Zone** is the area **outside the front door**.

This zone is focused on **arrivals and access**, not movement inside the home.

It typically includes:
- The **doorbell** (detects someone ringing the bell)
- A **remote lock box** (used for showings or service access)
- Exterior motion or camera coverage near the door

This zone helps the system understand that **someone is attempting to enter or leave**, without assuming they are already inside.

---

### Interior Zones

**Interior zones** are the spaces **inside the home**.

Common interior zones include:
- Entryway
- Kitchen
- Family room
- Bedrooms
- Hallways

Interior zones are used to understand **where someone is inside the house** and to control things like lights, audio, and notifications.

Each interior zone works independently, even if rooms are next to each other.

---

## Why the Difference Matters

The system treats these zones differently on purpose:

- Exterior zone = **intent** (approaching, ringing, unlocking)
- Interior zones = **presence** (someone is actually inside)

This prevents false triggers and keeps automations accurate.

---

## Zone Count & Setup Fees

Each zone requires setup and testing.

Because of this, **the more zones a property has, the higher the setup fee**.

More zones mean:
- More configuration
- More testing
- More automation logic

Most homes start with only the essential zones and add more later as needed.
