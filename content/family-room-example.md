---
title: "Family Room Example"
---

The family room is a common interior zone used to illustrate how the Smart Showing Kit is applied at the room level. It represents a typical shared living space where activity naturally occurs during a showing.

This example demonstrates layout, intent, and flow—rather than specific devices or technical details.

## Purpose of the Family Room Zone

The family room is selected because it is:
- A primary gathering space
- Frequently visited during showings
- Centrally located within the home

Monitoring this area provides meaningful context without intruding into private spaces.

## Room-Level Layout

Within the family room, system components are positioned to observe general presence and movement. Placement is designed to remain unobtrusive and blend naturally into the environment.

The focus is on:
- Detecting entry into the space
- Recognizing sustained presence
- Identifying activity outside expected timeframes

## Flow During a Showing

As visitors move through the family room during a showing, activity is interpreted as normal and expected. The system remains passive, allowing the space to be experienced without disruption.

No interaction with the system is required by the agent or visitors.

## Role in Vacant Protection

When the property is unoccupied, the family room zone becomes an important reference point. Activity detected in this space outside of expected windows is treated as significant and handled according to the system's mode.

This helps provide early awareness while avoiding unnecessary sensitivity.

## Relationship to Other Zones

The family room zone operates in coordination with:
- The entry / exit area
- Other interior zones

Together, they create a balanced view of how the property is accessed and used.
