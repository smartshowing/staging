The Smart Showing Kit is a managed system designed to support secure, remote property showings and continuous protection for vacant or staged homes.

It provides visibility, accountability, and early detection during the listing period—without requiring permanent installation, technical involvement from the seller, or changes to the property.

## Purpose

Traditional showings rely on physical presence, manual scheduling, and trust alone. When a property is vacant or staged, this creates gaps in oversight and increases exposure to risk.

The Smart Showing Kit is designed to close those gaps by introducing a lightweight, temporary system that supports:

- Remote showings
- Controlled access
- Interior awareness
- Continuous vacant-home protection

## How It's Used

The system is installed at the start of a listing or defined showing window and removed when no longer needed.

During this time, it operates in clearly defined modes that adapt to how the property is being used—whether actively shown, temporarily idle, or fully secured.

All monitoring and management are handled as part of the service.

## What This Is — and Is Not

**This is:**
- A managed showing and protection system
- Temporary and non-invasive
- Designed for vacant or lightly occupied listings
- Focused on awareness and accountability

**This is not:**
- A permanent security system
- A DIY smart-home installation
- A requirement for seller-managed apps or dashboards

## Who It Benefits

The Smart Showing Kit is intended for:
- Vacant listings
- Remote sellers
- Properties with limited on-site supervision
- Agents managing multiple showings or schedules

It is designed to support the listing process—not interfere with it.
