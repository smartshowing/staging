---
title: "How the System Works"
---

The Smart Showing Kit is designed to operate quietly in the background, supporting the showing process from arrival to exit while maintaining continuous awareness of the property.

The system follows a simple, predictable flow that aligns with how listings are actually shown.

## Arrival

When a showing begins, the system detects entry at the front of the property. This establishes a clear start point for the visit and confirms that access has occurred as expected.

No action is required from the seller. Entry is handled through approved showing methods and tracked automatically.

## Interior Awareness

Once inside, the system maintains awareness within key living areas. These interior zones are selected based on layout and use, focusing on primary spaces such as living rooms, family rooms, and common areas.

The system does not interfere with the showing experience. It operates passively, providing visibility without disrupting movement or privacy.

## During the Showing

While a showing is in progress, the system remains in a mode appropriate for interior activity. It distinguishes between normal movement and unexpected behavior based on context and timing.

This allows the property to be shown naturally while still maintaining accountability.

## Exit

When the showing concludes, the system detects departure from the property. This confirms that the visit has ended and that the home has been vacated as expected.

If the property is not exited within the expected window, or if activity occurs after exit, the system responds according to its configured mode.

## Between Showings

When the property is unoccupied, the system shifts into a protective state. During these periods, it focuses on early detection of unexpected entry or activity.

This ensures the property remains protected not only during scheduled showings, but also between them.

## Continuous Operation

Throughout the listing period, the system operates continuously without requiring adjustment from the seller. Modes are managed as part of the service and adapted to how the property is being used.

The result is a consistent, predictable experience that supports both showings and vacant-home protection.
